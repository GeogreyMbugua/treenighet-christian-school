About us

A Christ-centred school in the heart of Namelok

We nurture children to grow in faith, knowledge and character – a centre of excellence for families across Loitokitok, Kajiado County.

Our story

Named for the Trinity

“Treenighet” is the Norwegian word for Trinity. Our name is a daily reminder that our school is built on faith in the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

We follow Kenya’s Competency Based Curriculum, while placing Christian faith, good character and care for one another at the heart of school life.

Our vision

To raise a generation of God-fearing, knowledgeable and responsible young people who transform their communities.

Our mission

To provide holistic, quality education grounded in Christian values, nurturing each child spiritually, academically and socially.
