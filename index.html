About us

A school built on the Trinity

“Treenighet” is the Norwegian word for Trinity. Our name reminds us that everything we do is rooted in faith in the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

We partner with parents and the Namelok community to give children strong academic foundations, good character and a love for God and one another.
