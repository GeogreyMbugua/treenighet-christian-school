Admissions

Welcome to the Treenighet family

We are glad you are considering Treenighet Christian School for your child. Joining us is simple – here is how it works.

How to apply

Four simple steps

Our office is happy to guide you through each step. Fees, term dates and available places are available on request.

Start an enquiry

  1. Get in touch

    Call, message us on Facebook or send an enquiry through our contact form.

  2. Visit the school

    See our classrooms, meet our teachers and ask any questions you have.

  3. Complete the application

    Fill in the admission form and provide the documents listed below.

  4. Welcome to Treenighet

    We confirm your child’s place and help them settle in from day one.

What to bring

Documents for admission

Please confirm the full list with the school office when you apply.

  • Copy of your child’s birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Latest school report, if transferring from another school
  • Child’s immunisation or clinic card
  • Parent or guardian’s ID and contact details
Transport

School van service

Our school van carries pupils between home and school around Namelok. Ask the office about routes, pick-up times and availability for your area.
